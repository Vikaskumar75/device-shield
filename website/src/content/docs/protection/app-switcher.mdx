---
title: App-switcher protection
description: Hide your app's content in the app switcher and Recents screen.
---

When the user opens the app switcher, the operating system shows a snapshot of
your app. App-switcher protection keeps sensitive content out of it:

```dart
await DeviceShield.setAppSwitcherProtection(true);
```

## iOS

The SDK covers the window with a blur just before the app leaves the
foreground, so the snapshot shows the blur, and removes it when the app
becomes active again. Verified on the iOS Simulator.

## Android

Android uses the same `FLAG_SECURE` flag as
[screenshot protection](../screenshot/), which hides the Recents thumbnail.
While app-switcher protection is on, screenshots are blocked too. The flag
stays set until both protections are off.
